OVERVIEW

What the film is about

Brief Make a Father's Day film that says something most ads don't bother to: that the most ordinary-looking dads are quietly the most heroic people we know — someone worth animating like an anime superhero.
Approach Landed on a Jujutsu Kaisen–inspired anime style after testing several looks, then generated every frame ourselves — pushing angle, lighting, and expression until an ordinary father in a cape actually felt cinematic.
Result A fully AI-generated short film — storm-lit superhero reveal, quiet study-desk scenes, and warm daily-life moments like grocery runs and cooking — completed and posted in under three days.
PROCESS

How it came together, day by day.

STEP 01

Finding the style

Tried several visual directions before landing on a JJK-style anime look — the right balance of dramatic and warm for a father-as-superhero story.

STEP 02

Locking character consistency

Generated dozens of angle, lighting, and expression variations of the same character with Nano Banana to keep him recognisable across every single shot.

STEP 03

Building the shot sets

Organised the film into four sets — the storm-lit reveal, the study desk, the sunrise bicycle ride, and the quiet home scenes — and generated each as its own visual arc.

STEP 04

Motion with Veo 3

Brought the still frames to life scene by scene, iterating on motion and timing until the pacing felt right.

STEP 05

Voice & final cut

Script and voiceover written by Rohan and Niraj, laid over the final edit before posting to Instagram and LinkedIn.
